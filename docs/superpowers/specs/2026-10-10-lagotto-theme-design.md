# The Lagotto theme

## Goal

A third selectable look, **Lagotto** — curly Lagotto Romagnolo dogs hunting
truffles in an oak wood — next to Kronika and Kurczaki. Approved mockups:
the "LifeRPG – motyw Lagotto" design canvas
(https://claude.ai/artifact/8wbNSjvd7vSxpexvHdx7i4): home, login, Zadania,
the "Wygląd" picker and the badge detail are drawn on the cream ground;
**the chosen ground is the golden one** (the "Tło: złoty" artboard). The
cream and forest grounds and the other tints were explored and dropped, so
there is no colour sub-choice: Lagotto is one `AppThemeId`, exactly like
Kurczaki.

## Decisions

- **Depth:** full dressing, like Kurczaki — palette, fonts, ornaments and
  illustrations. Polish labels never change.
- **Picker:** a third tile, "Lagotto" / *loki, trufle, dębowy las*.
  Storage, default and the `app_theme` key are unchanged (`lagotto`).
  Tiles now sit two a row (three abreast is too narrow for a preview's
  band label on a phone) and the sheet scrolls.
- **Motif:** a leather dog collar. Bands are chocolate leather with a
  dashed stitch line near each long edge; the card's inner frame is a
  dashed stitch; badges are dog tags hanging from a split ring.
- **Third shape, not a third bool:** `RpgDecor.eggBadges` and
  `eggMedallion` become enums (`BadgeShape { medal, egg, boneTag }`,
  `MedallionShape { ring, egg, paw }`), so each new look adds a case
  instead of another flag. Kronika and Kurczaki keep exactly their
  current drawings.
- **Perches rest on the frame**, never over a band's label (the rule from
  9d54b4e): the dog on the character card lies on the card's top edge, to
  the left of the edit button.

## Fonts

- **Fraunces** (display): static instances cut from the variable font
  with fontTools (`SOFT` 100, `WONK` 0, `opsz` 36; wght 600 / 700 / 800),
  subset to Latin + Latin Extended. The Polish diacritics (ą ć ę ł ń ó ś
  ź ż, „ ” …) were checked in each file's cmap when cutting them — widget
  tests render with a stand-in font, so they cannot check glyphs.
- **Nunito** (body) — already bundled for Kurczaki; the mockups' Nunito
  Sans is not worth a second body family.

## Illustrations (`assets/lagotto/`, `LagottoArt`)

Three coats: *bianco* (off-white with brown ears and patches), *marrone*
(solid brown), *arancio* (orange puppy). Every outline is a ring of small
arcs, which reads as a curly coat. Assets: `dog_face`, `dog_face_tongue`,
`dog_lie` (bianco, eyes closed), `dog_sniff` (marrone, head down),
`dog_sit` / `dog_sit_brown`, `puppy` / `puppy_white`, `truffle`,
`truffle_gold`, `basket`, `paw`, `oak_leaf`, `oak_leaf_brown`, `oak_tree`,
`ground`. They are generated by `tools/theme_art/lagotto_art.py` (the
script that drew the mockups) and committed as plain SVG files; the
script is kept so the art can be re-tuned.

## `LagottoDecor` hooks

| Hook | Lagotto |
|---|---|

| `cornerOrnament` | oak leaf |

| `loginEmblem` | sitting dog, tongue out |

| `perch` | character card: lying dog + truffle basket; quests summary and board quest: orange puppy; daily quest: sitting brown dog (left); achievements summary: white puppy |
| `footerScene` | home: sniffing dog, truffle, puppy; quests: sleeping brown dog, basket, puppy; achievements: sitting dog beside a golden truffle |
| `questsTitleMascot` | small sitting dog |
| `xpRunner` | orange puppy |
| `goldIcon` | golden truffle |
| `fabFace` | dog face with tongue on a light-orange disk |
| `traitLeading` / `traitValue` | paw / value on a biscuit bone (≤ 3 characters; longer legacy text stays text) |
| `badgeShape` | `boneTag`: a bone cast in the tier's metal, an accent bone inset carrying the emblem, hung from a split ring |
| `badgeNest` | none |
| `badgeHero` | the tag, sitting dog left, puppy right |
| `medallionShape` | `paw`: the count on the paw pad, the pad filling with orange over the day (paler while pending, dashed once claimed) |
| `countEgg` | a round orange dog tag with the count |
| `backdrop` | honey gold with a fine curl pattern |

Stitching needs one new hook, `bool get stitchedBands` (false in the
base), read by the `Stitched` wrapper around `TopBand`, `BottomBand` and
the quests summary band. AppBars are not stitched.

The change-request FAB with a face (Kurczaki, Lagotto) now fills with the
theme's `actionFill` instead of a hard-coded chick yellow (`#FFD23F` →
Kurczaki's `#FFC83D`, a barely visible shift).

## Testing

- `test/theme/lagotto_theme_test.dart` — mirrors the Kurczaki test: home,
  Zadania, login, gallery and badge screens render in Lagotto without
  layout errors and with their illustrations; every asset exists; the bone
  and paw-pad paths stay inside their boxes. Both theme tests share
  `test/theme/theme_test_support.dart`.
- `test/features/theme_picker_sheet_test.dart` — the third tile exists and
  applies; three tiles fit a 360 dp phone, two a row.
- The Kronika/Kurczaki badge and medallion tests keep passing unchanged
  after the bool → enum change.
